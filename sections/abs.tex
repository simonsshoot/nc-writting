\abstract{With capabilities expanding rapidly, large language model (LLM)-driven agents are being deployed in high-stakes domains such as finance, healthcare, and industry, which bring significant risks. Mainstream defense frameworks rely on static hard-coded rules or predefined tools, limiting their generalization to evolving or unknown scenarios. Inspired by the human capacity for continual self-reflection and knowledge refinement, we propose DEFEND, a self-evolving defense framework. It generates executable Python guardrail tools in response to the runtime behavior of agents and treats these tools as evolvable artifacts that are continuously updated through lifelong interaction. Meanwhile, an independent auditing mechanism ensures the safety of each evolved tool, enabling the framework to maintain trustworthiness and consistency while dynamically evolving. The framework demonstrates excellent performance across various domains, promoting unification in the field of agent defense.}
