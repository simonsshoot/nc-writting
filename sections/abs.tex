\abstract{With capabilities expanding rapidly, large language model (LLM)-driven agents are being deployed in high-stakes domains such as finance, healthcare, and industry, which bring significant risks. Mainstream defense frameworks rely on static hard-coded rules or predefined tools, limiting their generalization to evolving or unknown scenarios. Drawing upon the human capacity for cognitive evolution, which involves continuously abstracting experience from ongoing interactions and refining coping strategies into structured knowledge, we propose EVOLVE, a self-evolving defense framework. It generates executable Python guardrail tools in response to the runtime behavior of agents and treats these tools as evolvable artifacts that are continuously updated through lifelong interaction. Meanwhile, an independent auditing mechanism ensures the safety of each evolved tool, enabling the framework to maintain trustworthiness and consistency while dynamically evolving. Extensive experiments across three benchmarks spanning multiple domains utilizing four backbone LLMs, demonstrate EVOLVE's excellence, promoting unification in the field of agent defense.}
